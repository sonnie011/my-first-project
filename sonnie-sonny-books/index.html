Meet the author

Sonnie Sonny: an author writing from lived experience

Hello, I’m Sonnie Sonny. Writing is deeply personal to me. The books After the Tears and The Long Way to Joy grew out of my own journey through infertility and miscarriage—the difficult seasons, the heartbreak, the questions, and the strength it took to keep moving forward.

These are more than stories I wanted to tell; they are part of my life. I wrote them to give a voice to experiences that can feel lonely, and to offer understanding, comfort and hope to anyone walking a similar road. My journey has shaped the woman I am today, and sharing it is one way I hope someone else feels seen and less alone.

Alongside these personal books, I create journals and practical digital resources to encourage growth in everyday life. Thank you for being here, taking the time to read, and supporting my work.
