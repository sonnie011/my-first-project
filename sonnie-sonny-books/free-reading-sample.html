PREFACE

To the Woman Holding This Book

If this book has found its way into your hands, I believe it is not by accident.

Perhaps you are grieving the loss of a baby. Perhaps you have experienced one miscarriage, or maybe several. Perhaps you are still waiting and wondering when God will answer your prayers. Or maybe you are reading this because someone you love is walking through this painful journey, and you want to understand how to support them.

Whatever brought you here, I want you to know one thing: You are not alone.

For a long time, I believed I was the only one carrying this silent pain. I smiled in public while my heart was breaking in private. I listened to questions I could not answer and endured judgments from people who did not know my story. I wrestled with grief, fear, disappointment, and the difficult question of "Why?"

But through every tear, every unanswered prayer, every doctor's appointment, every miscarriage, and every season of waiting, God never left me.

This book is not just about my pain. It is about God's faithfulness in the middle of pain.

It is about hope when hope seems impossible.

It is about choosing faith over fear.

It is about learning that our value is not measured by motherhood but by the unconditional love of God.

As you read these pages, you may cry with me. You may recognize parts of your own story in mine. You may finally find words for emotions you have carried silently for years. My prayer is that you will also find healing, encouragement, and the courage to keep trusting God.

This book is also for husbands, families, friends, and church communities. I hope it helps them understand that behind every miscarriage is a grieving mother, behind every season of infertility is a family longing for hope, and behind every smile may be a heart carrying unimaginable pain.

May these pages inspire us all to choose compassion over judgment, encouragement over criticism, and prayer over gossip?

Most importantly, I pray that when you reach the final page, you will know that God has not forgotten you. His plans are still good. His promises still stand. His love never fails.

Thank you for allowing me to share my story with you.

May God bless you, comfort you, and strengthen your heart as we walk this journey together.

With love, faith, and hope,

INTRODUCTION

There was a time when I believed becoming a mother would happen naturally.

Like many women, I imagined getting married, starting a family, decorating a nursery, and holding my first baby within a few years. I never imagined that my journey to motherhood would include doctors' offices, medications, emergency rooms, countless tears, and heartbreaking goodbyes.

If someone had told me that I would endure years of infertility, be diagnosed with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), suffer three miscarriages, and face life-threatening complications during pregnancy before finally holding my healthy baby, I probably would not have believed them.

But that became my reality.

There were moments when I felt broken beyond repair.

Moments when I wondered why my body seemed to fail me.

Moments when I questioned whether I would ever hear someone call me "Mama."

Every negative pregnancy test felt like another loss.

Every miscarriage stole not only a baby but also a future I had already imagined.

There were days when I avoided baby showers because they reminded me of everything I longed for.

There were nights when I cried myself to sleep while asking God questions that seemed to have no answers.

And yet...

This is not a story about defeat.

It is a story about choosing hope when despair seemed easier.

It is about discovering strength when I thought I had none left.

It is about a husband whose love never wavered.

It is about doctors who refused to give up.

It is about faith that survived even when my heart was breaking.

And ultimately, it is about a God who kept His promises in ways I could never have imagined.

If you are reading this while walking through infertility, pregnancy loss, or another painful season, I want you to know something before you turn the next page: You are not alone.

I cannot promise your journey will look exactly like mine because every story is different. But I can promise that your pain is real, your tears matter, and your life has value regardless of where you are on your journey.

This book is not written because I have all the answers.

It is written because I survived.

And if my story can help even one woman believe that hope is still possible, then every painful memory shared within these pages will have served a greater purpose.

So take my hand.

Let me tell you how it all began.

CHAPTER ONE

A Girl with Big Dreams

When I look back on my childhood, I smile.

There was nothing extraordinary about where I came from. I was simply a girl from Nyeri with a heart full of dreams and a quiet belief that one day I would build a beautiful life.

Like many young girls, I imagined growing up, pursuing an education, finding love, getting married, and eventually becoming a mother. Those dreams seemed so natural and so certain that I never stopped to wonder whether life might have a different plan for me.

My childhood was filled with simple joys, family, school, and the ordinary experiences that shaped who I would become. Looking back now, I realize how precious those carefree years were. I had no idea that life would one day ask me to become stronger than I ever thought possible.

In 2007, after completing my examinations, I packed my bags and left Nyeri for Nairobi to begin a new chapter of my life. I had been admitted to college, and like many young people leaving home for the first time, I was excited, nervous, and hopeful.

Nairobi felt like a city where dreams could come true.
